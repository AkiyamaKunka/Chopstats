A refreshed iOS look, a backup in your own iCloud, and a way to correct a meal with a note.

• A refreshed iOS look on every screen: Apple's system colors and text styles throughout, buttons that dim when pressed instead of rippling, and a tab bar without the colored pill. The app also follows Increase Contrast.
• iCloud Backup, in Settings › Your Data: your meals, weight, measurements, workouts and meal pictures are copied to a Chopstats folder in your own iCloud Drive. After a reinstall, or on a new iPhone signed in to the same iCloud account, Chopstats offers to restore them. It is on by default, but a notice comes first, and nothing is uploaded until you answer it. API keys are never included, and the developer receives nothing.
• Correct with a note, in Edit meal: tell your AI what was different, such as "only half the rice" or "add a fried egg", then tap Correct with AI. The answer fills in the foods and numbers, and nothing is saved until you tap Save. Your note is sent with that meal's description, foods, numbers and earlier notes, never its photo, and it stays with the meal.
• A photo taken before 4 a.m. also carries a short summary of the day before's meals, so leftovers from a late dinner are recognized. If you already use Chopstats, it asks you before it sends this, or a note, for the first time.
• Export Data now includes your meal pictures, and Import Data can open that file directly (Choose File…).
• Body: the Log sheet has a Date row, so you can add a weigh-in you missed.
• If you log meals by hand without an API key, the app now opens on Today instead of Settings.
• Your own server: Chopstats sends nothing to an address that does not start with https://, and your upload key goes only to the server it was saved for.
• Fixes: a shared file the app can't read, such as an AVIF photo or a file over 25 MB, now gets a message saying it was skipped and why, instead of being dropped silently; a photo you edit in Photos after logging it is not logged again; when your AI account runs out of credit, Watch Camera Roll pauses and says why instead of sending the same photos again; Meta glasses food photos checked in the background are no longer skipped as "not food"; Restaurant nutrition keeps answers with several items or links, and recognizes more chain names; places you marked are removed from your phone after 180 days; the end of a list no longer hides under the Save or + button; many screens read better at large text sizes and with VoiceOver.
